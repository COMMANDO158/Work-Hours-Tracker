FlowFocus · App icon

Seven marks to choose from

Each mark is shown large, on a light and a dark home screen, under the round mask Android applies, and at the small sizes it lives at most of the time. A is the icon you have now.

A

A. Work dial

The icon you have now: the Topaz work dial on black, kept here to compare against.

B

B. Brilliant, side view

One Topaz stone, crown over pavilion, in the same pose as the gem of the day. The plainest gem mark.

C

C. Brilliant, top view

The same stone seen from above. Its facets radiate like a dial, so it reads as both gem and clock.

D

D. Dial with a gem hub

Today’s icon, with a small faceted Topaz stone where the black hub was. The least change.

E

E. Week rosette

Seven wedges, Saturday to Friday, one per weekday gem, like the Insights rosette. Tuesday and Friday are short: rest days.

F

F. Emerald-cut timer

A step-cut Topaz whose dark table fills with light to 3h 45m of the 6-hour day. Gem and timer are one shape.

G

G. Facet hourglass

Time flowing through glass: the last of the top bulb runs down onto a faceted heap. A nod to “Flow”.

An installed icon can’t move or catch the light, so the glass is drawn as flat facet tones taken from the Topaz ramp in the app, with one fixed sparkle where it helps. The week rosette uses each weekday’s own gem colour.
